About Me

Storytelling through video & strategy

I'm a video editor and social media manager with a background in engineering and a passion for the outdoors. I bring an analytical mindset and a creative eye to every project — whether it's a short-form Reel, a YouTube edit, or a full content strategy.

I work primarily in DaVinci Resolve and have hands-on experience producing and editing content across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. I understand what makes content perform — and I build it with purpose.

Based on a smallholding in Gauteng, I'm available for freelance and employed work with local businesses and brands across South Africa.
